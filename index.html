Eyesight Screening

Check your eyesight in about five minutes

Letters are sized for your own screen, and your camera keeps you at exactly the right distance.

  • A bank or ID card
  • A tape or string
  • A bright room

This is a screening estimate, not a medical diagnosis. Results depend on your screen, lighting and distance. If you have any concern, see an eye doctor.

Match your card to the screen

Take a bank or ID card and hold it flat against the screen with its short side across the screen. Drag the slider until the bar below is exactly as wide as the short side of your card.

Teach the camera your distance

Measure 50 cm with your tape from the camera to your eyes. Sit still at that distance, look at the camera, then press Calibrate.

Colour vision test

  • Set the screen to full brightness. Turn off night mode, blue-light filter and dark mode.
  • Use daylight or a white light, and take off any tinted glasses.
  • Type the number you see in each circle, then press OK. If you see no number, press Nothing.
  • Answer quickly and don't stare. The first plate is a practice.

This checks the most common red-green type only, on a phone screen. It is a screening, not a medical colour test.

Estimate nearsightedness

Hold the phone at arm's length with both eyes open and glasses off. Slowly bring it closer until you can read all 5 tiny letters clearly, then stop and press the button.
