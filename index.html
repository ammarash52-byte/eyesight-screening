👁️ Eyesight Screening

⚠️ This is a screening estimate only, not a medical diagnosis. Results depend on your screen, lighting and distance. If you have any concern, see an eye doctor.

The test takes about 5 minutes. You will need a credit / ATM / ID card and a tape or string.

Step 1 - Calibrate the screen

Hold the card flat against the screen. Move the slider until the blue bar is exactly as wide as the card.

Card width

Step 2 - Calibrate the camera

Measure 50 cm with your tape from the camera to your eyes. Sit still at exactly that distance, look at the camera, then press Calibrate.

Starting camera...

Step 3 - Get in position

  • Sit in a bright room, light behind or beside you, no glare on the screen.
  • Set the screen to maximum brightness.
  • Take your glasses off.

Colour blindness test

  • Set the screen to full brightness. Turn off night mode / blue-light filter / dark mode.
  • Use normal daylight or a white light. Take off any tinted glasses.
  • For each circle, type the number you see, then press OK. If you see no number, press Nothing.
  • Answer quickly - don't stare. The first plate is a practice plate.

⚠️ This checks the most common red-green type only, on a phone screen. It is a screening, not a medical colour test.

Nearsightedness estimate

Hold the phone at arm's length with both eyes open, glasses off. The letters are very small. Slowly bring the phone closer until you can read all 5 letters clearly, then stop and press the button.
